rsIDs are converted to GRCh38 positions with the Ensembl REST service; positions are looked up directly.
What the numbers are
  • Typed = genotypes called directly from the Illumina GSA array, after QC (file FULL_QC_FINAL). Counts are genotypes in 1,256 people; HWE p from plink --hardy. 1000G frequencies are given for all 26 populations.
  • Imputed = Michigan Imputation Server (1000G phase 3 30x GRCh38, Eagle 2.4, Minimac 4.1.6), kept if R² ≥ 0.80 and MAF ≥ 0.001; no further MAF cut. Counts are best-guess genotypes. 1000G frequencies for the 5 superpopulations. Variants that are also on the array are marked "also typed".
  • Every frequency is the frequency of a named base on the GRCh38 + strand. The GRCh38 reference sometimes carries the rare or risk allele (e.g. rs2476601: REF A = risk allele), so "ALT" is not "minor" and not "risk".
Flags on typed SNPs
  • ok array bases match 1000G as written.
  • complemented the array writes the Illumina TOP strand; bases were complemented to the + strand. Normal for about half of all SNPs (checked: Uzbek-vs-EUR correlation is identical to direct matches, 0.907 vs 0.906).
  • ambiguous strand A/T or C/G SNP: the complement is the same pair, so the strand cannot be told from the bases. The comparison with 1000G assumes the bases as written; 42 of 1,615 such SNPs look reversed (frequency f vs 1−f). Check before use.
  • 1000G multi-record 1000G has more than one SNV record at the position; no 1000G frequency given.
  • not in 1000G / base mismatch no comparable 1000G SNV.
